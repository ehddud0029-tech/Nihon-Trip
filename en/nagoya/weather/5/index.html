Frequently asked questions

What's the weather like in Nagoya in May?
Warm and pleasant for sightseeing. The average high is 25°C and the low 15°C, with 150 mm of rain in the month.
What should I wear in Nagoya in May?
Short or thin long sleeves, with a light layer for the evening. Mornings and evenings are much cooler than daytime, so bring a layer that's easy to take off and put on. It rains often this month, so bring a folding umbrella.
