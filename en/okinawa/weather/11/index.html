Frequently asked questions

What's the weather like in Okinawa in November?
Warm and pleasant for sightseeing. The average high is 25°C and the low 20°C, with 119 mm of rain in the month.
What should I wear in Okinawa in November?
Short or thin long sleeves, with a light layer for the evening. The sun is strong, so bring sunscreen and sunglasses. Great walking weather—comfortable sneakers are all you need.

November travel notes

Mood
Peak autumn leaves. Kyoto temples hold evening illuminations.
Popular now
Kyoto autumn leaves, Tokyo's ginkgo avenues, and the start of year-end illuminations.
Good to know
Autumn-leaf spots are busiest on weekend mornings. Aim for early weekdays.
