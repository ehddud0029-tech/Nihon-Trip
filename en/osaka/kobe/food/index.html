Getting around Kobe

MainJR, Hankyu, Hanshin and the subway all meet at Sannomiya. For Harborland, use JR Kobe Sta. or the Kaigan subway line

BusThe 'City Loop' sightseeing bus — circles Kitano Ijinkan, Sannomiya and Meriken Park

OtherFor Nunobiki Herb Gardens take the ropeway from Shin-Kobe Sta.; for Port Island, the Port Liner

On footSannomiya, Motomachi (Nankinmachi) and Meriken Park are a 20-min walk
