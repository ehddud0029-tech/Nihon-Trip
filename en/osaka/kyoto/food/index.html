Getting around Kyoto

MainKarasuma subway line — Kyoto Sta., Shijo, Karasuma-Oike (north–south); Tozai Line — Nijo Castle (east–west)

MainKeihan — Gion-Shijo, Fushimi Inari; Hankyu — Kawaramachi to Arashiyama

BusFor places far from stations like Kiyomizu-dera and Ginkaku-ji, take city buses (including the EX100/EX101 sightseeing express from Kyoto Sta.)

PassSubway and bus 1-day pass. Buses are always crowded, so mix in trains and walking
