Nearby sights

  • Shinsekai · TsutenkakuRetro sign-lined alleys and Tsutenkaku Tower, packed with kushikatsu shops.
  • Abeno Harukas 300The observatory atop one of Japan's tallest buildings, with a 360° view over the Osaka plain.
  • Shitennoji TempleSaid to have been founded by Prince Shotoku as Japan's first state temple, with a five-story pagoda and cloisters.
  • Tennoji Park · TenshibaA park with the 'Tenshiba' lawn, a zoo and an art museum, connected directly to Shinsekai.
