January festivals

Kobe Luminarie

神戸ルミナリエ

A festival of light begun in memory of the victims of the Great Hanshin Earthquake, with huge illuminated arcades.

Area
Osaka · Kobe
When
Late January – early February (about 10 days)
Location
Former Foreign Settlement · Higashi Yuenchi Park
Travel tips · Since 2024 it has been held in January instead of December.
