Nara Park · Deer

A vast park where over a thousand deer roam freely. You can feed them deer crackers.

Todai-ji Temple

東大寺

A giant bronze Buddha sits inside the Great Buddha Hall, one of the world's largest wooden buildings.

20 min walk from Kintetsu-Nara Sta.

Kasuga Taisha

春日大社

A shrine with vermilion corridors lined by 3,000 bronze and stone lanterns, and a beautiful forest approach.

10 min by bus from Kintetsu-Nara Sta.

Isuien · Yoshikien Gardens

A Japanese garden that 'borrows' Todai-ji's Great South Gate as scenery — a peaceful place to rest.

Kofuku-ji Temple

興福寺

A temple at the entrance to Nara Park, famous for its five-story pagoda and the Buddhist statues in its National Treasure Hall.

5 min walk from Kintetsu-Nara Sta.

A World Heritage temple with some of the oldest surviving wooden buildings in the world.
