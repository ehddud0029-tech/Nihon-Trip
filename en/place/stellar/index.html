Tax-free shopping (new system from November 2026)

  • From November 1, 2026, Japan's tax-free system became a refund system. You pay the price including consumption tax at the store.
  • Spending ¥5,000 or more (before tax) at one tax-free shop qualifies. Show your passport at the till.
  • Within 90 days of purchase, have customs at the airport or seaport confirm you are taking the goods out of Japan.
  • After that, the store refunds the consumption tax (by credit card, for example). Refund methods and fees vary by store.
  • At Narita, Haneda, Kansai, Chubu, Fukuoka, New Chitose and Naha airports you can also do the customs check online with Visit Japan Web.
