Frequently asked questions

What's the weather like in Shikoku in December?
Chilly winter weather. The average high is 12°C and the low 4°C, with 47 mm of rain in the month.
What should I wear in Shikoku in December?
Coat and sweater; a light down jacket on chilly days. Mornings and evenings are much cooler than daytime, so bring a layer that's easy to take off and put on. It gets chilly after sunset, so a light scarf comes in handy.
