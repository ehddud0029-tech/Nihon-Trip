Shopping tip

Shopping tipGift shops cluster in GRANSTA inside the gates and Tokyo Okashi Land outside them. Handy for buying everything on your last day.

Opening hoursDepartment stores are usually open 10 a.m.–8 p.m.; discount stores like Don Quijote stay open late into the night.

PaymentMost shops take cards and IC cards, but cash is easier at markets and stalls. Bring your passport for tax-free shopping.

Shopping

Shopping streets & markets in Tokyo Station & Kanda, Tokyo · 3

TAXFREE

Tax-free shopping changes from November 1, 2026

Instead of tax being deducted at the store, you now pay including tax and get it refunded later.

  1. Show your passport at a tax-free store and pay the tax-inclusive price
  2. When leaving Japan, customs at the airport checks your passport and purchases
  3. Once checked, the store (or a refund agent) refunds the consumption tax

Minimum spend and refund methods can vary by store, so ask before paying.
