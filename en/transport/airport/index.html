🚆Japanese transport: all you need to know
Getting around Japan is almost entirely by rail and subway. Dedicated trains run from airports to city centers, and within cities a single IC transit card covers subways, buses and even convenience-store purchases. Between cities, the shinkansen and limited express trains get you there fast.
IC transit cardsSuica/PASMO (Tokyo), ICOCA (Osaka), SUGOCA (Fukuoka), Kitaca (Hokkaido) and others are interchangeable. Okinawa's OKICA is separate. You can also add them to the iPhone Wallet app.
Airport → cityAfter choosing an area, check the fastest airport train or bus for each city in the 'Getting there from the airport' box at the top.
Getting between citiesTokyo ↔ Osaka is about 2.5 hours by shinkansen; Hakata ↔ Yufuin/Beppu is around 2 hours by limited express. Between Okinawa's islands, you fly.
💡Handy tips
  1. Tokyo and Osaka subways are packed at rush hour (7–9 a.m., 5–7 p.m.). Avoid them if you have big luggage.
  2. Taxi base fares are fairly high, so they're best for short trips or groups.
  3. If you're traveling between several cities or visiting many sights, a regional pass may be cheaper. Compare it with your itinerary.
  4. Avoid phone calls on trains, and set your phone to silent near priority seats.
